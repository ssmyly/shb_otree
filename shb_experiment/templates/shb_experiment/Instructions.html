Study Instructions

Please read carefully. We will ask you a short comprehension question at the end.

How the Study is Structured

The study has four short stages, in this order:

Practice (warm-up) → Calibration round → Work round 1 → Work round 2
  • Practice: a short warm-up so you can get used to the counting task. It does not affect your pay.
  • Calibration round: you complete the counting task once. The employer uses your performance here to set a starting wage offer (which does contribute to your pay). You do face the possibility of being rejected, getting 0 wage this period, if your performance is too low or your employer evaluates you unfavorably. The bar to clear here is lower than in the work rounds, however, since this round is just to figure out where you start.
  • Work rounds (2 total): in each one, you first decide how much to invest in training, then complete the counting task, then see your wage offer (always facing the possibility of rejection). Both work rounds count toward your bonus.
The Counting Task

You will see a target digit and a grid of numbers. Count how many times the target digit appears, type the number, and click Submit (or press the Enter key). You get instant feedback (✅ or ❌) and a new problem appears. You answer as many problems as you can in the time allowed.

Investing in Training (Work Rounds Only)

Before each work round, you may pay for training. Training does not add points to your score directly. Instead, it makes the task easier by shrinking the counting grid, so you can answer more problems in the same amount of time.

How Wages are Set
1
After the task, the employer reads your performance score with a small amount of random noise added (between {{ noise_min }} and +{{ noise_max }} points). This represents the fact that employers can't observe your true productivity perfectly.
2
If that noisy reading is at or above the bar to clear that round ({{ threshold_baseline }} points in calibration; {{ threshold_work }} points in each work round), you are hired and receive a wage offer. Otherwise your wage that round is 0 (training cost is still taken out).

How You Get Paid
  • Each round's net earnings = wage − training cost (never below 0).
  • Your total bonus is the sum of net earnings across the calibration round and the two work rounds.
  • You also get a ${{ participation_fee_usd }} base payment for completing the study.
  • Bonus points are converted to dollars at {{ points_per_usd }} points = $1.
Quick recap of the flow:
  1. Practice round (warm-up, no pay).
  2. Calibration round (counting task → starting wage offer).
  3. Work round 1: pick training tokens → counting task → see your wage.
  4. Work round 2: same as work round 1.
  5. Final summary and a short exit survey.
